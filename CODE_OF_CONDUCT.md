# Code of Conduct

HolyScreen is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or belief.

## Expected behavior

- communicate with empathy and respect;
- welcome questions and constructive disagreement;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- respect privacy and project boundaries;
- accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, intimidation,
publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately through the contact information on the
[sharkwedy profile](https://github.com/sharkwedy). The maintainer may remove
content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
